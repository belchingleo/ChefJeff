# Level 1 — Steak

A 14×9 practice kitchen split by a short wall into a prep area on the left and a cooking area on the right, open below the wall. The prep area has the beef fridge at the top and three boards in a column. The cooking area has a sink, one stove and a stack of two clean plates along the top; the right wall holds an extinguisher, a counter, the serving window and a bin; dirty returns and spare counters run along the bottom. Chefs spawn near each area's center with randomized assignments.

One pot starts on the stove. Each counter slot holds one item. Off-stove pots do not heat. Shared pickup, throwing, catching, plating, carrying, washing, collision and sprint rules apply.

## Orders

Five steak orders arrive every 24 game seconds, each with 90 seconds of patience. The round lasts up to 180 game seconds, about 4 minutes at the default 0.75× clock. Each steak earns 30. Finish three orders with at least 60 operating income and no more than two bad reviews to complete the level. Two plates mean a served plate must come back through dirty returns and the sink before a third steak can be plated.

A steak is raw beef from the fridge, chopped on a board, cooked in the pot on the stove and plated on a clean plate. It burns 10 seconds after it is cooked if left on the heat, and a burnt pot catches fire 8 seconds later. Global preparation, burning and penalty rules apply.

## 中文说明

# 第一关：牛排

牛排练习厨房。开局前或结算后选择第一关。

## 布局与资源

14×9 厨房，一道短墙把左侧备菜区和右侧烹饪区隔开，墙下方相通。备菜区上方是牛肉柜，三块案板竖排成一列。烹饪区上方依次是水槽、唯一的灶台和两只干净餐盘；右墙有灭火器、柜台、出餐口和垃圾桶；下方是脏盘回收和备用柜台。两名厨师分别在两个工作区中央附近出生，身份随机分配。

只有一口锅，开局在灶台上。每格柜台只容纳一个物品。锅离灶不加热。双方共用全部取放、抛食材、接物、装盘、端锅、洗碗和冲刺规则。

## 订单

共 5 份牛排，每 24 游戏秒到一单，每单耐心 90 秒，总时长 180 游戏秒。默认 0.75 倍游戏时钟，未提前结束时约 4 分钟现实时间。每份 30 元；目标为出餐 3 单、净收入至少 60 元、差评不超过 2 次，达成即结算。只有两只盘，出第三单前必须把用过的盘子从脏盘回收拿到水槽洗净。

牛排的做法：从牛肉柜取生牛肉，在案板切好，放进灶台上的锅里煎熟，再装进干净盘出餐。熟了之后留在火上 10 秒会糊，糊锅再过 8 秒着火。加工、糊锅、处罚和餐具循环沿用全局规则。
